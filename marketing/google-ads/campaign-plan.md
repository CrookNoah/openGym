# Campaign Plan — Seasoned Tree Care

## Who the ads target

Homeowners (and some property managers) in your service area who are actively
searching for tree work. Three intents, in order of value:

1. **Urgent** — storm damage, tree on house, dangerous leaning tree. Highest
   value, least price-sensitive. They call the first credible company.
2. **Planned removal/trimming** — they know they need the work, they're
   comparing 2–3 quotes. Trust signals win: licensed & insured, certified
   arborist, reviews, free estimate.
3. **Researching** — "how much does tree removal cost". Lower intent; the
   keyword build below bids on cost queries cheaply because they often convert
   to quote requests.

The ad copy leans on the trust signals that decide this niche: **licensed &
insured, free estimates, certified arborist, local, fast response, cleanup
included**.

## Campaign settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign type | Search only (uncheck Display Network AND Search Partners) |
| Campaign name | STC — Search — [CITY] |
| Location | Your service area: your city + towns you'll actually drive to. Use "Presence: people in or regularly in" — NOT "interest" |
| Language | English |
| Bidding | Start: Maximize Clicks with a max CPC limit of ~$15. After 15–30 conversions: Maximize Conversions |
| Budget | $50–100/day to start ($1,500–3,000/mo). Tree service CPCs run $8–25; below ~$50/day you won't get enough clicks to learn anything |
| Ad schedule | 24/7 for Emergency ad group; business hours +2h for the rest if nobody answers the phone after hours |
| Assets (extensions) | Call asset (your number), location asset (link Business Profile), sitelinks (Free Estimate, Tree Removal, Storm Damage, Reviews), callouts (Licensed & Insured, Free Estimates, [YEARS]+ Years Experience, Cleanup Included), structured snippet (Services: Tree Removal, Trimming, Stump Grinding, Storm Cleanup) |

## Ad group structure

One ad group per service so the ad headline can mirror the search exactly
(this is what earns the click):

| Ad group | Intent it captures |
|---|---|
| Tree Removal | "tree removal near me", "tree removal cost" — the volume driver |
| Tree Trimming & Pruning | maintenance work, repeat customers |
| Emergency & Storm | urgent, 24/7, highest-value calls |
| Stump Grinding | cheap clicks, easy add-on jobs |
| Arborist / Tree Health | "certified arborist near me", sick/dying tree — quality leads |

Keywords for each are in `keywords.csv`, ads in `ads.csv`.

## Measuring success

A tree job is worth hundreds to thousands of dollars, so the math is forgiving
if you track it:

- Target cost per lead (call >30s or form): **$40–100** is healthy for this
  industry.
- Ask every caller "how did you hear about us" anyway — attribution is never
  perfect.
- Judge the campaign monthly on **booked jobs and revenue**, not clicks. One
  removal job typically pays for 1–3 weeks of ad spend.
